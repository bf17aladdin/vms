Option 2: With Pipeline Wrapper
─────────────────────────────────

Option 3: Multi-Camera Parallel
────────────────────────────────

   processor = get_async_processor()
   processor.add_camera("cam_1", "Front Door")
   processor.add_camera("cam_2", "Garage")
   
   results = await processor.process_frames_parallel({
       "cam_1": frame1,
       "cam_2": frame2
   })
